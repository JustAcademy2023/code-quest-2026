02 / the roadmap

  
Ninety days, three missions

  
No giant syllabus. Just three stages that turn curiosity into something you can show an employer.

  

    
DAYS 1 TO 30
Build the base
Pick one language, practise daily and write tiny programs. Cover variables, conditions, loops, functions and basic data structures.

    
DAYS 31 TO 60
Ship real projects
Build a to do app, a budget tracker or a small API. Break things, fix them and learn how to get unstuck on your own.

    
DAYS 61 TO 90
Polish and prepare
Write clear READMEs, deploy one project with a live link and practise explaining your work in mock interviews.
